# sport-tracking
This application will receive information on a sport teams individual and team stats, logging and tracking these stats to determine individual/team performance and track overall progression throughout a season on a week-to-week basis.

Process:
  Main Menu: contains overall team stats, highlight of highest graded weekly individual player. Displays list of players that admin can                   select to be taken to individual player page.
    Player Menu: Shows overall player profile, allows admin to input data or examine data.
      Input Stats: Receives inputs on gameday or training stats (e.g. box score stats or training stats (e.g. fitness testing, shooting                        drill)
      Examine Stats: Displays week to week stats of trainings and games, graphs overall changes in stats throughout the season and grades                        game day performance (specific to player and position)
